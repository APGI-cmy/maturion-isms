# Unchanged QA and convergence

Version: 1.1.0. Effective date: 2026-11-26.

The current learner-guide QA suite is the external source of truth. Resolve its published `Current.json` at every actual dispatch and handover, validate the relevant artifacts, and preserve the requirement binding used. Do not copy the suite into this agent's operating rules, weaken validators, change required evidence or manufacture approvals. Resolve the path through the private deployment profile using the approved logical QA-pointer binding. Observed creation-time release data remains local provenance and is not a portable public dependency.

Required course checks include the suite's actual current icons, cover/layout, source fidelity, research, teaching/outcome alignment, practice/feedback, accessibility and ordered duplication checks. Follow the live suite rather than treating this summary as exhaustive.

Duplication runs within the current LU, then against the previous LU, then across the course prefix. A stop blocks later stages. LU1 has no previous unit. Correct by preserving distinct teaching, removing redundant exposition, or using an explicit purposeful refresher/Stop and reflect link to the earlier precise location. Preserve the original source. Check the whole affected learner sequence, accessible equivalent and feedback, so a correction does not leave the same instruction immediately nearby.

## Freeze and dependency rules

Freeze only an actual independent passed check with exact evidence and its dependencies. Retain the earlier receipt when the component, applicable requirements, research and relevant cross-unit relationships remain unchanged. Recheck errors and affected dependencies only. Cover/style changes may affect rendering; changed teaching may affect duplication and outcomes. A new cross-unit conflict can require an affected comparison; it does not justify blanket re-review of unrelated frozen teaching.

Changes to job names, timestamps, output filenames, generated paragraph counters, transport packaging, status messages or publishing-only data do not create new pedagogical work. Distinguish content identity, requirement identity and packaging identity. Never put a record's own hash inside its hashed content. Bind immutable evidence to stable submission bytes; keep later status pointers outside that identity. A genuine changed requirement must be reconciled, not ignored as hash noise.

## Finite correction and stop-and-fix

The ordinary design/review chain allows four total rounds with cumulative counters across restart, recovery and correction. Preserve applied fixes and versions; do not oscillate to an earlier state. Consolidate findings for the full reviewed scope rather than releasing a trickle of previously visible defects.

After a fourth non-pass, Maturion obtains a separately preserved independent root-cause diagnosis: why was the result still wrong? A fifth round is eligible only under the recorded human policy and a verified decision that all remaining issues are genuinely natural evolution. Repeated findings, incomplete earlier fixes, missed required coverage, invalid evidence, inconsistent reviewers, transport faults, counter resets and regressions are administrative/process causes. Mixed, unknown or administrative causes halt for repair and escalation. An exceptional fifth does not reset any count; a fifth non-pass cannot trigger a sixth automatically.

A repair may recover the same saved candidate and model response without consuming a fresh design round. It still requires real evidence validation. Recovery is not permission to rerun a stopped chain after its budget is exhausted. Independent review of this agent bundle is separate from course QA. No QA-suite file is changed by agent creation or learning capture.

An accepted Course Creator registration must preserve the same QA source and controller-owned gates. Agent-adapter tests validate authority, binding, replay and route behaviour separately; they do not change the learner-guide QA suite. The new generic AIMC persona cannot substitute its prose for a real controller QA receipt.
